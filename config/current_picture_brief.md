Write the current picture as a compact daily analyst brief for readers who already follow the conflict.

The brief should answer five things clearly: what the operating baseline is, what changed in the latest reporting cycle, what did not change, what new deltas are emerging after the latest anchor, and what deserves attention over the next 12 to 24 hours.

Use paragraph form only. Each paragraph should carry concrete analytical content. Do not rely on headings, bullets, or scaffolding to create the appearance of structure.

Anchor reporting sets the frame. Supplementary briefings can shape emphasis. Stream deltas may sharpen or challenge the picture, but they should not dominate it.

The tone should be crisp and specific, not introductory. Avoid generic conflict language, recap boilerplate, or empty statements about uncertainty. If gaps remain, identify the actual gap.
